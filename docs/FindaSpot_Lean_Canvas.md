# FindaSpot — Lean Canvas

**Date:** 10/15/26
**Team:** 305Soft Team 17

---

## Problem

- Inability to find open parking spots when they arrive at a parking lot.
- Lack of knowledge regarding specific parking lot permit requirements or allowed parking times.
- Lack of real-time traffic and lot data for customers to plan their commute schedule effectively.

## Solution

- Color-coded interactive map showing lot availability.
- Permit and rule directory for each lot.
- Crowdsourced traffic updates & Google Maps integration. Plan with built-in AI.

## Unique Value Proposition

Instead of jumping around for 30 minutes every day, easily find a spot that works for you and your schedule.

## Advantage

SpotHero offers spot reservation services for events. For this app, we won't be doing spot reservation. Rather, we would be telling people if spots are free and lots have availability.

## Customer Segments

- Primary audience of college students and young professionals commuting multiple times a week.
- Drivers burdened by limited campus/city parking and time-sensitive schedules.
- Early adopters are student commuters at universities facing severe parking lot congestion.

## Key Metrics

- User reports of lot conditions (free parking spots, traffic conditions, etc.)

## Channels

Word of mouth from existing users and flyer advertisement. Intended for inbound users, through an indirect nature.

## Cost Structure

| Type | Costs |
|------|-------|
| **Fixed** | App store fees ($99/yr Apple, $25 Google), web domain, developer time |
| **Variable** | Google Maps API usage, hosting and database, Stripe fees (2.9% + $0.30) |
| **Other** | Gemini API and marketing |

## Revenue Streams

- **Institutional licensing:** Universities and cities can license parking analytics to understand parking demand.
- **Free/Premium users:** Free users get the main parking lot-finding functionality with a limited AI chat. Premium users get full AI chat, quicker reports, and parking analytics.
- **Local sponsors:** On-campus businesses and resources can advertise near close lots.
