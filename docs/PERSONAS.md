# Team 17 — Persona Cards

User personas for the FindaSpot parking app.

---

## 1. URI Commuter Student

### Demographic Information
- Age: 19
- Current student
- Home location: Charlestown, RI
- School location: Kingston, RI
- Non-resident
- Personal vehicle for transportation

### Pain Points and Needs
- Drives to school multiple days a week; needs commuter parking
- *"I often show up and cannot find a parking spot"*
- Drives to desired parking lot only for it to have no spots left
- Comfort with technology: 10/10

### Solutions
- Map showing amount of available parking spots in each lot
- Show commuter-only parking spots
- Save preferred lots
- Display peak times for specific lots

## 2. URI Resident Student

### Demographic Information
- Age: 21
- Current student
- Location: Kingston, RI
- URI resident
- Resident permit
- Personal vehicle for transportation

### Pain Points and Needs
- Needs a place to park their car throughout the day and night
- *"I run out to complete errands and come back to see my spot was taken"*
- The lot closest to their dorm is taken
- Comfort with technology: 10/10

### Solutions
- Show which lots are available closest to their dorm
- Save preferred lots
- Display peak times for specific lots

## 3. URI Staff/Faculty

### Demographic Information
- Age: 40
- Profession: Professor
- Home location: Warwick, RI
- School location: Kingston, RI
- Non-resident
- Personal vehicle for transportation

### Pain Points and Needs
- Drives to school every day of the week
- Wants to know where dedicated staff parking is located
- Wants to know where available staff parking is located near desired building
- Comfort with technology: 6/10

### Solutions

- Save preferred lots
- Display peak times for specific lots

## 4. Campus Visitor

### Demographic Information
- Age: 30
- Profession: Parent
- Location: Out-of-town
- Non-resident
- Personal vehicle for transportation

### Pain Points and Needs
- Unfamiliar with campus layout and parking lot locations
- Confused about visitor permit rules and parking hours
- Worried about getting a ticket or parking too far from building
- Comfort with technology: 6/10

### Solutions
- Show open parking spots nearest to event venues
- Clear display of lot rules, hours, and other details
- Streamlined UI tailored for moderate tech comfort
